Design System Strategy: Luminous Ledger - Atelier Edition

1. Overview & Creative North Star

Creative North Star: "The Digital Atelier"
This design system moves away from sterile, "SaaS-blue" defaults, embracing a sophisticated, editorial aesthetic that feels more like a high-end boutique or an architectural studio than a banking app. It balances professional financial rigor with a tactile, refined sensory experience.

2. Visual Principles





Editorial Precision: Generous whitespace, deliberate typography, and a structured grid that echoes modern print magazines.



Deep Contrast: Using a dark, saturated primary blue (#4132A6) against soft, layered backgrounds to create depth and focus.



Purposeful Color: Color isn't just decoration; it's a semantic tool for growth (green), outflow (indigo/red), and categorization (muted neutrals).



Soft Geometry: A consistent 8px - 16px corner radius that feels friendly but remains disciplined.

3. Color Palette





Primary (Signature Blue): #4132A6 - Used for primary actions, header cards, and active navigation states.



Secondary (Deep Slate): #0F1D26 - Used for heavy headings and high-priority text.



Accent (Growth Green): #038C5A - Dedicated to positive financial indicators and "Save" actions.



Muted Accent (Dusty Rose): #BF9288 - Used for subtle highlights and secondary visual elements.



Surfaces:





Base: #F8F9FB (Light grey-blue)



Card: #FFFFFF



Overlay: Subtle gradients and backdrop blurs (20px+) for a modern "glass" feel.

4. Typography (Manrope)





H1 (Display): 32px, ExtraBold, -2% tracking. For large financial totals.



H2 (Section): 20px, Bold. For page titles and major card headings.



Body (Standard): 14px, Medium. For transaction details and descriptions.



Label (Overline): 11px, SemiBold, Uppercase, +5% tracking. For metadata like "TRANSACTION DATE".

5. Component Guidelines





Cards: Use a subtle border (border-slate-200/20) combined with soft shadows or tonal backgrounds. Avoid heavy, dark shadows.



Buttons:





Primary: Full bleed #4132A6 with white text.



Secondary: Ghost buttons or light blue tints (#E0E7FF).



Inputs: Floating labels or minimal underlined styles to maintain the clean, "uncluttered" look.



Navigation: A bottom bar with high-contrast active icons and muted inactive states to keep the focus on content.

6. Interaction Model





Tonal Layering: When a user interacts with an element, use subtle scale transforms (98%) and opacity changes rather than heavy color shifts.



Transitions: Smooth, 300ms cubic-bezier eases for all screen transitions.
